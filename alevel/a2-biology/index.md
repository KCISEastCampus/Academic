**OxfordAQA 9610 · BL03–BL05.** Choose a lesson below and follow the suggested order. Each lesson includes explanations, worked examples, practice, hints and solutions; attempt independent tasks before opening the feedback.

Use the [AS prerequisite bridge](/alevel/a2-biology/prerequisites/) to repair a gap in prior knowledge.

## Paper 3: Populations and genes {#paper-3}

Begin with an ecological problem, connect photosynthesis and respiration to production, then study cycles, inheritance and evolution.

1. **[Ecology and populations](/alevel/a2-biology/ecology/)** — Choose sampling, interpret population patterns and justify a conservation decision
2. **[Photosynthesis](/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis/)** — Trace electrons, protons and carbon; predict a limiting-factor change
3. **[Respiration and measurement](/alevel/a2-biology/respiration/)** — Connect stages, interpret two respirometers and calculate a rate
4. **[Energy and production](/alevel/a2-biology/energy-production/)** — Define the efficiency denominator and evaluate agricultural trade-offs
5. **[Nutrient cycles](/alevel/a2-biology/nutrient-cycles/)** — Name the forms and microbial processes; explain eutrophication
6. **[Inheritance](/alevel/a2-biology/inheritance/)** — Derive a ratio and judge whether counts support it
7. **[Evolution and allele frequencies](/alevel/a2-biology/evolution/)** — Separate selection from drift and explain reproductive isolation

[Unit diagnosis, transfer tasks and scope](/alevel/a2-biology/paper-3/).

## Paper 4: Control {#paper-4}

Study detection, communication and response, then connect homeostasis, gene expression and gene technology.

1. **[Receptors and behaviour](/alevel/a2-biology/receptors/)** — Reflexes, taxis/kinesis, pressure detection and visual sensitivity versus acuity
2. **[Nervous communication](/alevel/a2-biology/nervous-communication/)** — Propagation, frequency coding, synapses, summation and inhibition
3. **[Muscles](/alevel/a2-biology/muscles/)** — Sliding filaments, ATP roles, antagonists and fibre types
4. **[Plant responses](/alevel/a2-biology/plant-responses/)** — Root/shoot curvature, IAA, ethene and ABA
5. **[Homeostasis](/alevel/a2-biology/homeostasis/)** — Feedback, blood glucose pathways and control of heart rate
6. **[Gene expression](/alevel/a2-biology/gene-expression/)** — Epigenetic effects, RNA interference, differentiation and stem cells
7. **[Gene technology](/alevel/a2-biology/gene-technology/)** — PCR, recombinant DNA, transfer, selection and expression

[Unit diagnosis, transfer tasks and scope](/alevel/a2-biology/paper-4/).

## Paper 5: Practical and synoptic skills {#paper-5}

Combine Paper 3, Paper 4 and AS knowledge in experimental design, data analysis and synoptic writing.

1. **[Experimental design · RP1–10](/alevel/a2-biology/experimental-design/)** — A method another student could follow and a justified evaluation
2. **[Data and statistical skills](/alevel/a2-biology/data-skills/)** — A calculation with an interpretable conclusion and stated limits
3. **[Synoptic scientific writing](/alevel/a2-biology/synoptic-writing/)** — A coherent English explanation using the question's conditions

[Unit diagnosis, transfer tasks and scope](/alevel/a2-biology/paper-5/).

## How to use a learning page {#learning}

1. Check the prerequisites. Read the bridge explanation if a term is unfamiliar.
2. Study the mechanism with its locations, directions and conditions. State what each arrow transfers: matter, energy or information.
3. Work through the example and explain why the method applies.
4. Answer the independent English task before opening its hint or solution. Chinese explanations are available through the language switch.
5. Identify the precise gap in your answer, then retry in a changed situation. Reading the answer is not evidence that you can produce one.

Use a notebook to record the page, question, first response, help used and revised response. For peer feedback, quote the sentence or step that is unclear and explain what you expected it to mean; do not submit classmates' personal information.

## The three papers {#papers}

The names below use **Unit/Paper 3, 4 and 5 together with BL03, BL04 and BL05**. The qualification website also numbers the three A2 papers within A2; the BL code identifies the intended unit.

| Unit | Scope | Structure in the supplied specification and 2025 papers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BL03 | §3.3 Populations and genes | 60 marks of short/long questions and 15 marks of structured continuous prose |
| BL04 | §3.4 Control | 60 marks of short/long questions and 15 marks of structured critical data analysis |
| BL05 | Units 1–4, practical and synoptic skills | 55 marks in practical contexts and 20 marks of structured synoptic prose |

Each is **90 minutes, 75 marks and 20% of the complete A-level**. These are source-based descriptions, not predictions of the next paper's topics. BL05 requires AS knowledge as well as A2 knowledge.

## Sources and boundaries {#sources}

The scope uses the supplied **9610 specification v5.1**, the OUP *International A Level Biology: AS and A Level* textbook, and **January and May/June 2025 BL03–BL05 question papers with their final v1.0 mark schemes**. Page references beside adapted tasks identify the pair actually consulted. Adapted questions and self-written questions are labelled; their teaching feedback is not an official mark scheme.

- [Official qualification and resource entry point](https://www.oxfordaqa.com/qualification/international-as-and-a-level-biology-9610/)
- [Official-domain specification v5.1 used for section numbering](https://www.oxfordaqa.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/oxfordaqa-a-level-biology-specification.pdf)
- [Specification linked by the current qualification page when checked on 15 October 2026](https://www.oxfordaqa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/oxfordaqa-international-as-and-a-level-biology-specification.pdf)

The last PDF identifies itself as **v4.2**, despite its URL date; that differs from the supplied v5.1. This course records that discrepancy rather than inferring an examination season from a filename. Confirm the applicable specification with your school for your entry season. The two supplied 2025 BL05 papers allocate 2 of the final 20 marks to QWC, while the specification wording says there are no specific QWC marks; use the instructions and mark scheme for the actual paper.

The [AS bridge](/alevel/a2-biology/prerequisites/) is targeted revision, not a complete AS course. Practical preparation and data analysis on a website do not replace carrying out RP1–10 under school supervision.
