Choose a unit below. Pure mathematics, statistics and mechanics are parallel parts of the subject; statistics and mechanics are not subsections of the pure course. Each available unit has its own starting point, practice and review route.

## Further Pure Mathematics · FM03 {#pure}

Complex numbers, proof, calculus, differential equations, geometry and matrices. Start with roots and polynomials, or choose a prerequisite recovery route.

[Enter the course](/alevel/a2-further-mathematics/)

## Further Statistics · FM04 {#statistics}

MGFs, estimators, confidence intervals and further tests. Start with a probability table and follow the course route through independent and mixed tasks.

[Enter the course](/alevel/a2-further-mathematics/statistics/)

## Further Mechanics · FM05 {#mechanics}

This course is not yet available on this site. The ordinary MA05 course and calculator guides are not substitutes for an FM05 course.

## Before you start {#prerequisites}

Revisit [AS Further Mathematics](/alevel/as-further-mathematics/) when needed. The lesson you choose gives more precise recovery links. These are learning routes, not a statement that every listed optional unit must be taken for a particular examination entry.
