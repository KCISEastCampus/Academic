OxfordAQA 9665 · FS2 / FM04. This is the beginning of a new course, with one sample lesson available. It is separate from [FM03 Further Pure](/alevel/a2-further-mathematics/) and ordinary Mathematics MA04.

## Start with a probability table {#start}

[Moment generating functions: find the mean and spread](/alevel/a2-further-mathematics/statistics/moment-generating-functions/) — Build a function from probabilities, use derivatives at zero, then compare transformations and independent sums.

You need probability-weighted averages and exponential differentiation. The lesson links to short prerequisite explanations; you do not need to memorise a new list of distribution formulas before starting.

## Where this course will go {#route}

Only the first step below is available here. The remaining steps are planned, not completed lessons.

1. **MGF foundations — sample available:** finite distributions, moments, transformations and independence.
2. **Continuous and standard-distribution MGFs — planned:** integral definition, existence ranges, Poisson, exponential and normal derivations, and sums.
3. **Estimators — planned:** sampling distributions, bias, consistency and relative efficiency.
4. **Estimation — planned:** mean confidence intervals, assumptions, sample size and interpretation.
5. **Further hypothesis testing — planned:** power, independent and paired means, variances and variance ratios, goodness of fit and association.

The testing route will need its own sample: interpreting data and choosing a test require different support from deriving an MGF.
