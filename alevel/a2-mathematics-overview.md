Choose a unit below. Pure mathematics, statistics and mechanics are parallel parts of the subject; statistics and mechanics are not subsections of the pure course. Each available unit has its own starting point, practice and review route.

## Pure Mathematics · MA03 {#pure}

Functions, algebra, calculus, numerical methods and vectors. Start with functions; use the route for mixed and later review.

[Enter the course](/alevel/a2-mathematics/)

## Statistics · MA04 {#statistics}

Probability models, sampling, estimation and hypothesis testing. Start with the Poisson model, or use the prerequisite links for an earlier topic.

[Enter the course](/alevel/a2-mathematics/statistics/)

## Mechanics · MA05 {#mechanics}

Build models of objects, forces and motion. Start with modelling and choose the next topic from the mechanics route.

[Enter the course](/alevel/a2-mathematics/mechanics/)

## Before you start {#prerequisites}

Revisit [AS Mathematics](/alevel/as-mathematics/) when needed. The lesson you choose gives more precise recovery links. These are learning routes, not a statement that every listed optional unit must be taken for a particular examination entry.
