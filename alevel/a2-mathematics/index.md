## Study a Topic

**[Functions, Composite Functions and Inverse Functions](/alevel/a2-mathematics/functions/)** · P2.1

Find domains and ranges, form composite functions and choose the correct inverse. Work through five examples and a graph, then try six practice questions, including piecewise functions and finite domains.

- [Domain and range](/alevel/a2-mathematics/functions/#domain-and-range)
- [Composite functions](/alevel/a2-mathematics/functions/#composite-functions)
- [Inverse functions](/alevel/a2-mathematics/functions/#inverse-functions)
- [Practise independently](/alevel/a2-mathematics/functions/#practice)

**[Modulus Functions and Transformations](/alevel/a2-mathematics/modulus-and-transformations/)** · P2.1

Compare modulus graphs, describe combinations of transformations and solve equations and inequalities, including modulus on both sides. Use seven worked examples, a graph and six practice questions.

- [Modulus graphs](/alevel/a2-mathematics/modulus-and-transformations/#modulus-graphs)
- [Combinations of transformations](/alevel/a2-mathematics/modulus-and-transformations/#combinations-of-transformations)
- [Equations and inequalities](/alevel/a2-mathematics/modulus-and-transformations/#equations-and-inequalities)
- [Practise independently](/alevel/a2-mathematics/modulus-and-transformations/#practice)

**[Algebraic Fractions and Algebraic Division](/alevel/a2-mathematics/algebraic-fractions-and-division/)** · P2.1

Simplify fractions, keep domain restrictions and find quotients and remainders. Work through six examples, then try five practice questions before moving to Partial Fractions.

- [Simplification](/alevel/a2-mathematics/algebraic-fractions-and-division/#simplification)
- [Operations with fractions](/alevel/a2-mathematics/algebraic-fractions-and-division/#operations-with-fractions)
- [Algebraic division](/alevel/a2-mathematics/algebraic-fractions-and-division/#algebraic-division)
- [Practise independently](/alevel/a2-mathematics/algebraic-fractions-and-division/#practice)

**[Partial Fractions](/alevel/a2-mathematics/partial-fractions/)** · P2.1 and P2.7

Learn to express algebraic fractions in partial fractions, including repeated factors. Use them in integration, then try four practice questions.

- [Learn the method](/alevel/a2-mathematics/partial-fractions/#method)
- [Repeated factors](/alevel/a2-mathematics/partial-fractions/#worked-example-2--a-repeated-factor)
- [Use the worked examples](/alevel/a2-mathematics/partial-fractions/#worked-examples)
- [Practise independently](/alevel/a2-mathematics/partial-fractions/#practice)

**[Binomial Series](/alevel/a2-mathematics/binomial-series/)** · P2.2

Expand negative and fractional powers, combine series and use them for approximations. Work through six examples and six practice questions, with checks on the range of validity and accuracy.

- [The binomial series](/alevel/a2-mathematics/binomial-series/#the-binomial-series)
- [Partial fractions and products](/alevel/a2-mathematics/binomial-series/#partial-fractions-and-products)
- [Approximations](/alevel/a2-mathematics/binomial-series/#approximations)
- [Practise independently](/alevel/a2-mathematics/binomial-series/#practice)

**[Trigonometric Functions and Formulae](/alevel/a2-mathematics/trigonometric-functions-and-formulae/)** · P2.4

Use inverse and reciprocal functions, prove identities, and solve equations with compound and double angle formulae. Work through nine examples, reciprocal graphs and eight practice questions.

- [Inverse functions](/alevel/a2-mathematics/trigonometric-functions-and-formulae/#inverse-trigonometric-functions)
- [Reciprocal functions](/alevel/a2-mathematics/trigonometric-functions-and-formulae/#reciprocal-trigonometric-functions)

- [Compound angle formulae](/alevel/a2-mathematics/trigonometric-functions-and-formulae/#compound-angle-formulae)
- [Sine and cosine forms](/alevel/a2-mathematics/trigonometric-functions-and-formulae/#sine-and-cosine-forms)
- [Double angle formulae](/alevel/a2-mathematics/trigonometric-functions-and-formulae/#double-angle-formulae)
- [Practise independently](/alevel/a2-mathematics/trigonometric-functions-and-formulae/#practice)

**[Exponential and Logarithmic Functions](/alevel/a2-mathematics/exponential-and-logarithmic-functions/)** · P2.5

Model growth and decay, use logarithm laws with the correct domain, and solve equations or find inverses. Work through eight examples, inverse graphs and seven practice questions.

- [Growth and decay](/alevel/a2-mathematics/exponential-and-logarithmic-functions/#exponential-growth-and-decay)

**[Differentiation](/alevel/a2-mathematics/differentiation/)** · P2.6

Choose and combine differentiation rules, find tangents and normals, classify stationary points, and differentiate implicit and inverse functions. Work through nine examples and eight practice questions.

**[Parametric Equations](/alevel/a2-mathematics/parametric-equations/)** · P2.3 and P2.6

- [Cartesian equations](/alevel/a2-mathematics/parametric-equations/#cartesian-equations)

**[Integration — Choosing a Method](/alevel/a2-mathematics/integration/)** · P2.7

**[Integration — Trigonometric Integrals and Applications](/alevel/a2-mathematics/integration-applications/)** · P2.7

**[Differential Equations](/alevel/a2-mathematics/differential-equations/)** · P2.8

Separate variables, use given conditions and form models for natural growth and decay. Work through six examples, then try six practice questions, including half-life and constant solutions.
