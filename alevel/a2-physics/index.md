**PH03 — Fields and their consequences.** Start with Circular Motion below. This course builds on [AS Physics](/alevel/as-physics/).

**Looking up a formula?** Open [PH03 Quick Reference](/alevel/a2-physics/quick-reference/).

## Start Learning

Study these lessons in order. Each has worked examples, practice, hints and solutions, with practice adapted from OxfordAQA papers.

1. **[Circular Motion](/alevel/a2-physics/circular-motion/)** — Angular speed, acceleration towards the centre, force diagrams and contact conditions.
2. **[Simple Harmonic Motion](/alevel/a2-physics/simple-harmonic-motion/)** — The SHM condition, motion and energy graphs, springs, pendulums and period derivations.
3. **[Gravitational Fields and Satellites](/alevel/a2-physics/gravitational-fields-and-satellites/)** — Field strength, potential, graph gradients, circular orbits and orbital energy.
4. **[Electric Fields](/alevel/a2-physics/electric-fields/)** — Charge signs, field and potential, combined fields, parallel plates and particle deflection.
5. **[Capacitance](/alevel/a2-physics/capacitance/)** — Charge and energy, graph gradients, plate geometry and dielectric changes.

Start with the explanations if this is new to you. For revision, go straight to the **Practice** section of your chosen lesson and keep the solutions closed.
