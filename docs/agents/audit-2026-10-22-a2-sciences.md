## Prioritised findings

Priority describes the repair order within this audit: **P1** affects evidence for a core learning outcome or a usable independent retry; **P2** affects recovery, synthesis or the reading route; **P3** is an editorial improvement. It is not a site-wide correctness severity rating.

### 1. P1 — Photosynthesis promises electron/proton tracing without a matching independent output

**Current evidence.** The opening promises separate carbon, electron and proton paths ([EN photosynthesis:19](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:19); [ZH:20](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:20)). The membrane explanation and finished schematic supply these paths ([EN:43–51](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:43); [ZH:44–52](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:44)). P1 checks carbon accounting; P2–P5 check CO₂ restriction, gas exchange, chromatography and compensation, with no independent task requiring reconstruction of the membrane electron/proton pathway ([EN:61–77, 109–185](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:61); [ZH:62–78, 110–186](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:62)).

**Student consequence.** A student can finish the supplied tasks while still reversing proton movement or confusing electron transfer with carbon input. The diagram explains the mechanism but is not itself evidence that the student can use it. This is a sampled target-to-task gap under standard lines 15–21 and AGENTS lines 74–75.

**Specific repair.** Retain the finished teaching figure. Add a separate incomplete membrane task asking the student to trace electrons from water to NADP, label both proton directions and explain a blocked ATP-synthase step. Supply staged hints, a separate solution and a changed-condition retry. Record each promised tracing outcome against its actual task; do not infer coverage from the number of P tasks.

### 2. P1 — Several changed-condition retries reveal the judgment they should test

**Current evidence.** RC's “Analysis and retry” gives the 3 s time-origin retry and immediately derives its answer in the same details block ([EN:340–361](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-physics/capacitor-charge-and-discharge.md:340); [ZH:344–365](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-physics/capacitor-charge-and-discharge.md:344)). Its graph retry says to reverse the positive direction, then directly states the signs and unchanged quantities ([EN:459–461](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-physics/capacitor-charge-and-discharge.md:459); [ZH:463–465](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-physics/capacitor-charge-and-discharge.md:463)). Chemistry's buffer reattempt changes NaOH to 28.0 cm³ but immediately announces complete neutralisation and rejection of the buffer ratio ([EN acids:343–345](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-chemistry/acids-bases-and-buffers.md:343); [ZH:344–346](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-chemistry/acids-bases-and-buffers.md:344)).

**Student consequence.** Reading feedback exposes the new task's key decision before the student can make it. Closing the block afterwards does not restore an unseen transfer test. These remain useful explanations, but do not establish independent repair under standard lines 55–67.

**Specific repair.** Put each retry prompt outside the original solution, and put its check in a separate closed block. Keep all necessary accessible descriptions. Biology photosynthesis P5 already separates a changed scenario from its answer ([EN:169–187](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:169); [ZH:170–188](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:170)); reuse that local pattern. Biology unit-route retries also need a visible prompt after the original answer is closed, even where their checks are already separate.

### 3. P2 — Prerequisite recovery is less specific than the difficulty it sends students back to fix

**Current evidence.** Circular motion sends forces, vectors and Newton's second law to the general AS index ([EN:20](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-physics/circular-motion.md:20); [ZH:22](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-physics/circular-motion.md:22)). That destination starts with four different guided lessons and then reference notes ([AS index:12–25](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/as-physics/index.md:12)). Thermodynamics says uncertain Hess-law knowledge should return to the course prerequisite check ([EN:21](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-chemistry/thermodynamics.md:21); [ZH:22](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-chemistry/thermodynamics.md:22)); that check asks enthalpy sign reversal but contains no two-path Hess reconstruction ([EN Chemistry index:45–67](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-chemistry/index.md:45); [ZH:46–68](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-chemistry/index.md:46)). Circular-motion Q1's feedback diagnoses weak explanations, but the end of the page offers a general checklist and next lesson rather than a linked recovery/retry for that error ([EN:242–244, 425–427](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-physics/circular-motion.md:242); [ZH:244–246, 427–429](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-physics/circular-motion.md:244)).

**Student consequence.** Links exist, but the student must locate the missing skill and decide whether it has been repaired. The Hess sign question alone does not diagnose the cycle reasoning demanded next. This falls short of the precise recovery route in standard lines 29, 37 and 65–71; it is not a broken-link claim.

**Specific repair.** Point vectors/force balance to an existing relevant section or diagnostic, distinguishing a reference note where that is all that exists. Add a small same-endpoints/two-path Hess bridge before Born–Haber work. Attach one closed-answer retry to each identified conceptual difficulty, with a specific return anchor. Reuse Chemistry's existing error-route tables rather than building a tracking system.

### 7. P2 — Bilingual task support is inconsistent, and ordinary mechanism pages lack a visible bridge to English output

**Current evidence.** Acids Q5–Q7 are English tasks in EN but entirely Chinese prompts in ZH, including the weak-acid calculation and indicator explanation ([EN:362–400](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-chemistry/acids-bases-and-buffers.md:362); [ZH:363–401](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-chemistry/acids-bases-and-buffers.md:363)); ZH Q8 returns to English. These self-written prompts are not identified as a different language-training route. In ZH Biology photosynthesis, P2/P3 retain English questions but the hints and checks provide Chinese conceptual support only ([ZH:110–146](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:110)); this source does not offer a partially supported English mechanism answer before independent production. By contrast, synoptic writing explicitly offers an English sentence frame and later removes it ([ZH writing:86–140](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-biology/synoptic-writing.md:86)).

**Student consequence.** The language demand changes between adjacent questions without an explained learning purpose. Understanding a Chinese mechanism and independently expressing it in English are different outcomes. This does not establish a numerical/content mismatch; it identifies support inconsistency under AGENTS line 90 and standard line 77.

**Specific repair.** Retain the English task alongside Chinese explanation in Q5–Q7, preserving numbers and conditions. In a selected mechanism lesson, add a short English causal-link completion, then keywords, then an unframed explanation with feedback on entities, direction and pronouns. Link directly to the relevant writing section; a language switch alone is not scaffolding. Apply this selectively by learning need, retaining the stronger existing writing sequence.

### 8. P3 — Author provenance and internal labels sometimes interrupt student action language

**Current evidence.** Thermodynamics headings retain T1–T4 and acidity A1–A4 ([EN thermodynamics:42, 104, 180, 317](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-chemistry/thermodynamics.md:42); [ZH:43, 105, 181, 318](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-chemistry/thermodynamics.md:43); [EN acids:41, 103, 178, 279](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-chemistry/acids-bases-and-buffers.md:41); [ZH:42, 104, 179, 280](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-chemistry/acids-bases-and-buffers.md:42)). Biology repeatedly exposes P0–P5 identifiers in task and feedback labels ([EN photosynthesis:21–35, 61–75](/D:/GitHub/Academic/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:21); [ZH:22–36, 62–76](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis.md:22)). The ZH Chemistry index says “你提供的” papers ([index:86](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-chemistry/index.md:86)); ZH writing says “用户提供的” and gives a dense QWC/source discrepancy paragraph before its first worked mechanism ([writing:36–43](/D:/GitHub/Academic/zh/alevel/a2-biology/synoptic-writing.md:36)).

**Student consequence.** Some labels describe production/source administration rather than what the student should do. QWC interpretation can matter to students, but the source-history discussion delays the first activity. Stable question numbers and exam codes remain useful and are not themselves defects.

**Specific repair.** Lead with meaningful actions such as “trace the carbon” or “choose the correct equilibrium”; retain stable IDs/anchors and compact task identifiers where needed for reference. Replace conversational author-facing provenance with neutral source wording. Keep a short actionable exam note in the route and move the fuller version/source discrepancy to its existing reference area, without hiding uncertainty or deleting source identity (standard line 35).
