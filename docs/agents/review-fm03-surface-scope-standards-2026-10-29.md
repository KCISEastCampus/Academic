$$\begin{aligned}
S&=2\pi\int_0^{\frac12\ln3}(2t)(2\cosh(2t))\,\mathrm dt\\
 &=2\pi\int_0^{\ln3}u\cosh u\,\mathrm du\\
 &=2\pi[u\sinh u-\cosh u]_0^{\ln3}\\
 &=2\pi\left[\frac43\ln3-\frac53-(-1)\right]
 =\frac{4\pi}{3}(2\ln3-1).
\end{aligned}$$
