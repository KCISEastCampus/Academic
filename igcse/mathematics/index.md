# 🔢 Number


## Structure and calculation


- **Index laws** for multiplication and division, including **fractional powers**.
- Calculate exactly with **surds**: manipulation and simplification, including **rationalising the denominator**.
- **Standard form**: $$A \times 10^n \quad (1 \le A < 10)$$
- **Sets**: use notation n(A), A′, A ∪ B, A ∩ B, ξ; understand and use **Venn diagrams** to solve problems.
- **Upper and lower bounds**: calculate and use.

## Fractions, decimals and percentages


- Convert between fractions and **recurring decimals**.
- **Reverse percentage problems**.
- **Compound interest formula**: $$P\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n$$

## Ratio and proportion


- **Exponential growth and decay**.

## ✅ Number Quick Check


1. Rationalise $$\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$$. **(√3/3)**
2. Find the upper bound of 4.6 correct to 1 dp. **(4.65)**
3. Write 0.00045 in standard form. **(4.5 × 10⁻⁴)**

# 🔤 Algebra


## Notation and manipulation


- Transform **complex formulae**, including when the subject appears **twice**.
- Expand products of **two or three binomials**.
- Factorise quadratics of the form **ax² + bx + c**, including difference of two squares.
- Index laws including **fractional powers**.
- Manipulation of rational expressions: + − × ÷ for algebraic fractions with **linear or quadratic** denominators.
- Algebraic **proofs**.

## Functions, graphs and calculus


- **Function notation** f(x) = …; terms **domain** and **range**; composite function **fg** and inverse function **f⁻¹**.
- Equation of a line through two given points, or through one point with a given gradient.
- Gradients of **perpendicular** lines.
- Sketch and interpret **exponential** functions y = kˣ (k > 0) and **trigonometric** functions y = sin x, y = cos x, y = tan x for angles of any size.
- **Differentiation**: gradient function dy/dx of kxⁿ (n a positive integer or 0) and the sum of such functions; simplify expressions first where needed.
- Gradient of a function = gradient of the **tangent** at that point; work out the equation of a tangent at any point on a curve.
- Use differentiation to find **stationary points**: maxima, minima, points of inflection; sketch a curve with known stationary points.
- Deduce **roots** algebraically and **turning points by completing the square**; use the quadratic formula.
- Calculate or estimate **gradients of graphs** and **areas under graphs** (incl. quadratic and other non-linear graphs); interpret results (e.g. distance–time, velocity–time graphs).
- Express **direct and inverse variation** in algebraic terms; find unknown quantities.

## Solving equations and inequalities


- Solve quadratics by **completing the square** and the **quadratic formula**: $$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$
- Solve **simultaneous equations**: one linear and one quadratic; find approximate solutions using a graph.
- Solve linear inequalities in **one or two variables**, and **quadratic** inequalities in one variable.
- Represent solution sets on a number line **and on a graph** — dashed line for strict inequalities, solid line for included boundaries.

## Sequences


- **Quadratic sequences**: deduce the nth term (and other expressions).

## ✅ Algebra Quick Check


1. Factorise 2x² + 7x + 3. **((2x + 1)(x + 3))**
2. Find fg(x) if f(x) = x + 1 and g(x) = x². **(x² + 1)**
3. Differentiate y = 3x² + 4x. **(dy/dx = 6x + 4)**
4. Find the stationary point of y = x² − 4x. **(Minimum at (2, −4))**

# 📐 Geometry and measures


## Properties and constructions


- **Circle theorems**: apply and use to prove related results:
  - angle subtended by an arc at the centre = twice the angle at the circumference
  - angle in a semicircle = 90°
  - angles in the same segment are equal
  - opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral sum to 180°
  - tangent at any point is perpendicular to the radius
  - tangents from an external point are equal in length
  - perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord
  - alternate segment theorem
- **Geometrical reasoning and proof**: use standard theorems to justify results.

## Mensuration and calculation


- **Surface area and volume** of spheres, pyramids, cones and composite solids, including **frustums** of pyramids and cones.
- Relationships between **lengths, areas and volumes in similar figures**.
- **Arc lengths, angles and areas of sectors** of circles.

**Formulae to know**:

| Formula | Use |
|---|---|
| $$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$ | Pythagoras (incl. 3D figures) |
| $$\sin\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$$ | trig (incl. 3D figures) |
| $$\cos\theta = \frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$$ | trig |
| $$\tan\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$$ | trig |
| $$\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$$ | sine rule |
| $$a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$$ | cosine rule |
| $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$$ | triangle area |

## Transformations, matrices and vectors


- **Combined transformations** and **enlargements by fractional and negative scale factors**.
- **Vectors**: notation; calculate and represent graphically the **sum**, **difference** and **scalar multiple**; commutative and associative properties of addition; solve simple 2D geometrical problems using vector methods.
- **Matrices**: multiplication of 2×2 by 2×2 or 2×1 matrices; multiplication by a scalar; the **identity matrix I**.
- Transformations of the **unit square** in the x–y plane represented by a 2×2 matrix: rotations of 90°, 180°, 270° about the origin; reflections in x = 0, y = 0, y = x, y = −x; enlargements centred on the origin.
- **Combination of transformations** using matrix multiplication.

## ✅ Geometry Quick Check


1. In a cyclic quadrilateral, what do opposite angles sum to? **(180°)**
2. Find the volume of a cone with radius 3 cm and height 4 cm (π = 3.14). **(⅓ × π × 3² × 4 = 37.7 cm³)**
3. State the cosine rule. **(a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A)**

# 📊 Statistics and probability


## Presentation and analysis


- **Histograms with unequal class intervals**, **cumulative frequency diagrams**, **box plots**.
- **Quartiles, inter-quartile range and percentiles**.

## Probability


- Calculate the probability of **independent combined events**, including using **tree diagrams**.
- For independent events A and B: $$P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$$
- Calculate **conditional probabilities**, including using tree diagrams and other representations.

## ✅ Statistics Quick Check


1. What is the inter-quartile range? **(Upper quartile − lower quartile)**
2. If P(A) = 0.4 and P(B) = 0.5, independent — find P(A and B). **(0.4 × 0.5 = 0.2)**
3. What does a cumulative frequency diagram show? **(Running total of data up to each value)**
