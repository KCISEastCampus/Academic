[A2 生物](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/) · [Paper 5：实验与综合能力](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/paper-5/) · <a href="/alevel/a2-biology/synoptic-writing/" hreflang="en">English</a>

综合回答（synoptic answer）要连接 AS 和 A2 的相关知识，解决当前问题。它需要明确的生物实体、准确的动作、适用条件，以及不超出证据范围的结论。列出很多主题名称，并没有建立这些连接。

若蛋白质、膜或基因表达不熟悉，先用 [AS 先修页](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/prerequisites/)回补。依次学习[机制范文](#mechanism)、[比较范文](#comparison)和[证据范文](#evidence)，再从[句型支架](#supported)过渡到[关键词](#keywords)和[独立写作](#independent)。检查后完成[诊断重试](#retry)。

所有题目、范文与数值资料均为 **Self-written / 自拟题**，数据明确为构造。范文示范推理，不是官方评分标准，也不代表必须凑够某个固定得分点数量。

## 按任务要求组织作答证据 {#approach}

先读指令词、界定系统，再选知识。明确是哪种细胞或生物、哪个区室、哪个方向、什么条件，再把性质连到过程和结果。“This causes it to change”没有说清对象和方向；“loss of potassium ions makes guard-cell water potential less negative”则可以逐项检查。

| 任务 | 读者需要看到的证据 | 常见缺环 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe 描述模式 | 方向、范围、例外和带单位的具体数字比较 | 没描述观察结果，反而先编出机制 |
| Explain 解释 | 有实体、位置和中间步骤的因果链 | 只说“需要更多能量”，没说 ATP 如何供应或使用 |
| Compare 比较 | 对同一维度同时说明两个对象，包括相关相似点 | 两段各说各的，没有对应比较 |
| Evaluate 评价论断 | 支持与冲突证据、方法限制、有根据的结论 | 泛泛列“多重复”“人为误差” |
| Design 设计实验 | 可操作变量、独立单位、控制理由、记录与分析 | 只列仪器，不说明读数含义 |

先列简短机制链，再写连贯句子。每根箭头都应能换成准确动词，如 binds、diffuses、hydrolyses、phosphorylates、inhibits 或 changes permeability。辨认箭头表示的是**物质移动、能量转移还是信息传递**。DNA 序列信息可决定蛋白质序列，但 DNA 不会变成蛋白质。呼吸转移化学储能，物质被重新排列，并非变成能量。

**QWC 的边界。**用户提供的两套 **9610 BL05 2025** 评分标准均对 Q6.1–6.3 整体设置 2 分 QWC，考查科学术语使用及清楚、合逻辑的表达：January **MS v1.0 Final p.17**，June **MS v1.0 Final p.16**。它们不是独立的拼写、标点和语法奖励。大纲概述却写不设专门 QWC 分值，存在已记录的冲突。应准确描述这两份实际试卷，不能推断所有未来试卷都有或都没有这 2 分；以实际试卷和目标考季说明为准。本页自拟题不分配 QWC 分数。

## 示范：跨 AS 与 A2 的机制链 {#mechanism}

<!-- task:writing-model-mechanism -->
**Self-written / 自拟题 · Worked mechanism**

A seedling lacks functional chlorophyll but can mobilise its stored carbohydrate and respire aerobically. Explain why it may grow initially and then fail to maintain growth in the light after its reserves are exhausted.
<!-- /task:writing-model-mechanism -->

**完整英文示范：**

> Stored carbohydrate in the seed can be hydrolysed to soluble sugars. These sugars provide respiratory substrates and carbon skeletons for the developing seedling. Glycolysis occurs in the cytoplasm, while later stages of aerobic respiration in mitochondria support further ATP synthesis. ATP hydrolysis is coupled to processes such as active transport and biosynthesis, allowing initial growth. Without functional chlorophyll, the seedling cannot sustain normal light-driven photosynthesis to replace the organic substrates it uses. As its reserves become exhausted, respiration and biosynthesis are increasingly limited by available substrate, so growth cannot be maintained. Light alone cannot replace the missing organic material or make the defective photosynthetic system functional.

AS 连接是酶催化水解、运输与合成；A2 连接是[光合](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis/)和[呼吸](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/respiration/)。范文说清储存物、产物、ATP 产生位置及用途，区分早期生长与后期耗竭，没有把“白苗会死”当解释。有机碳可以在呼吸中以二氧化碳形式离开系统，并非变成能量；表型的遗传解释见[遗传](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/inheritance/)。

注意条件：题干明确给了幼苗能动用储存物且可呼吸，因此可使用这些前提。不能仅由叶色推断具体突变、准确存活天数或某种酶缺陷。若人为提供外源有机碳，结论就需要调整。

## 示范：在同一维度比较 {#comparison}

<!-- task:writing-model-comparison -->
**Self-written / 自拟题 · Worked comparison**

Compare the roles of voltage-gated ion channels and the sodium–potassium pump in a neuron's membrane. Relate their properties to ion movement and action potentials.
<!-- /task:writing-model-comparison -->

**完整英文示范：**

> Both voltage-gated ion channels and the sodium–potassium pump are membrane proteins that selectively affect ion movement across the neuron's membrane. An open channel allows its ions to move down their electrochemical gradient without directly using ATP for each transported ion. Voltage changes alter the opening of voltage-gated channels: sodium entry through open sodium channels causes rapid depolarisation, and potassium exit through open potassium channels contributes to repolarisation. In contrast, the sodium–potassium pump uses ATP to move three sodium ions out and two potassium ions in against their respective gradients. The pump maintains the concentration gradients needed for repeated signalling over time; it does not directly produce each rapid depolarisation or repolarisation. Thus channels change permeability during an impulse, while the pump helps sustain the ionic conditions in which impulses can occur.

这段把 AS 膜运输连接到[神经传递](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/nervous-communication/)。每组比较维度一致：相对梯度的运输方向、ATP 使用、作用时段。没有把通道等同受体，也没有把消耗 ATP 的泵说成每次快速电位变化的直接原因。动作电位之前如何检测刺激，见[感受器](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/receptors/)。

比较表可以帮助构思，但连续论述任务仍需用句子写出连接。“A uses ATP; B does not”只有在 A、B 和所指过程都明确时才有用。

## 示范：评价证据，不把代理当证明 {#evidence}

<!-- task:writing-model-evidence -->
**Self-written / 自拟题 · Worked evidence task**

Equal fresh masses from three independent tubers of each of two plant species are extracted into equal buffer volumes. Equal extract volumes are assayed at the same pH, temperature and final hydrogen peroxide concentration, using a pilot-validated early linear interval. Initial oxygen-production rates are A: 5, 6, 7 cm³ min⁻¹ and B: 8, 9, 10 cm³ min⁻¹. Evaluate the claim that species B contains 50% more catalase than species A.
<!-- /task:writing-model-evidence -->

**完整英文示范：**

> The mean initial oxygen-production rate is 6 cm³ min⁻¹ for A and 9 cm³ min⁻¹ for B, a 50% increase relative to A. The sampled B extracts therefore show greater catalase-associated activity under these assay conditions. Standardising tissue mass, extraction volume, pH, temperature and substrate concentration makes this comparison more informative. However, the assay measures enzyme activity rather than catalase concentration directly. Differences in extraction efficiency, enzyme activity per molecule or inhibitors in the extracts could contribute to the rate difference. There are only three independent tubers per species, and the sample ranges alone are not a significance test. Further independent sampling and a validated measurement of catalase amount would be needed to support the specific claim of 50% more enzyme. The current results support a difference in measured activity in these samples, not that exact difference in enzyme quantity throughout either species.

计算结果属于观察，酶含量则是对观察的解释。控制条件减少了一部分替代解释，却没有测量提取回收率或酶丰度。生物重要性、统计显著性与机制是不同判断。可回顾 [RP1](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/experimental-design/#rp1)、[数据推断](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/data-skills/#inference)和[基因表达](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/gene-expression/)。

**Self-written / 自拟题 · 完整方法写作示范。**更可靠的活性比较可随机抽取每种植物的多个独立块茎，把等鲜质量组织研磨进等体积同一缓冲液。统一提取和过滤流程、提取液体积和最终底物浓度，在控温水浴中平衡后混合。短时间间隔记录氧体积，估计早期线性斜率，并将块茎作为独立单位；同份提取液反复测量属于技术重复。加入无提取液空白检查非酶分解，随机安排测量顺序，比较块茎级速率及不确定度。漏气可能使读数偏低，须先检查；佩戴护目镜，使用教师配制的稀过氧化氢。该方法估计每单位鲜组织质量的相对酶活性；若要测酶量，需要另一项经验证的测量，不能只把同一活性结果说得更肯定。

## 第一步：保留部分句型支架 {#supported}

尝试前先理解机制。干旱信号中的 ABA 可促进保卫细胞净流失离子。周围条件可比时，保卫细胞水势变得较不负，水经渗透离开，膨压下降有助于气孔关闭。气孔开度下降可在适当条件下限制二氧化碳扩散与碳固定，同时减少失水。这是有条件的权衡，不是说每次气孔关闭都会使全部光合立刻停止。详见[植物响应](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/plant-responses/)与[光合作用](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis/)。

<!-- task:writing-supported -->
**Self-written / 自拟题 · Complete the mechanism**

Complete the paragraph in English, then add one sentence explaining a benefit and a cost to a plant during drought: “After ABA promotes net ion loss, guard-cell water potential becomes ____. Water moves ____ the guard cells by ____, reducing their ____. Stomatal closure reduces the diffusion of ____ into the leaf, which can limit ____.” Assume the water potential of the surrounding cells is initially unchanged.
<!-- /task:writing-supported -->

<details markdown="1">
<summary>提示——把溶质变化连到水分方向</summary>

溶质流失使水势变得较不负。要写出具体气体和过程，不用笼统的“less food”。

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>解答——有支架的机制题</summary>

> After ABA promotes net ion loss, guard-cell water potential becomes less negative. Water moves out of the guard cells by osmosis, reducing their turgor. Stomatal closure reduces the diffusion of carbon dioxide into the leaf, which can limit carbon fixation in photosynthesis. The response helps conserve water during drought but can reduce the production of organic compounds needed for growth.

检查方向：水通过部分透性膜由较高水势向较低水势净移动。该响应有保水收益，也可能降低碳同化；不是单个细胞有目的地作选择。

</details>

## 第二步：只保留关键词 {#keywords}

肌肉中 ATP 有多种具体用途，不能只写“运动需要能量”。ATP 与肌球蛋白结合使其从肌动蛋白解离；ATP 水解为下一次横桥循环准备肌球蛋白头。舒张时，ATP 依赖的泵把钙离子运回肌质网。这些过程连接蛋白质形状、运输、[肌肉收缩](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/muscles/)和[呼吸](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/respiration/)。

<!-- task:writing-keywords -->
**Self-written / 自拟题 · Keywords only**

A muscle fibre's ATP supply falls severely while some actin–myosin cross-bridges are attached. Explain two immediate consequences for cross-bridge cycling and relaxation. Use these keywords if helpful, but write a connected account: ATP binding, detachment, calcium ions, sarcoplasmic reticulum. Do not claim that ATP makes the actin and myosin filaments shorter.
<!-- /task:writing-keywords -->

<details markdown="1">
<summary>提示——找出两个不同的 ATP 依赖事件</summary>

一个直接涉及肌球蛋白与肌动蛋白；另一个涉及钙离子跨膜运输。

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>解答——关键词题</summary>

> ATP binding is needed for myosin to detach from actin, so a severe shortage can leave attached cross-bridges unable to detach normally and disrupt further cycling. ATP-dependent transport of calcium ions back into the sarcoplasmic reticulum also slows, delaying the fall in cytoplasmic calcium that permits relaxation. These are distinct uses of ATP; neither requires the actin or myosin filaments themselves to shorten.

检查是否区分了循环受阻和舒张延迟。“没有 ATP 就立即舒张”漏掉了这两个 ATP 依赖过程。

</details>

## 独立的结构化综合写作 {#independent}

写之前关闭范文，用简短提纲标出每步所在层级：DNA、RNA、蛋白质、细胞过程、个体或种群。以下任务不给固定句型，也不虚构官方分值。

<!-- task:writing-independent-gene -->
**Self-written / 自拟题 · Independent synthesis A**

Two plant lines have the same catalase coding sequence. Under the same stated growth conditions, line R has less catalase mRNA and a lower initial oxygen-production rate in a standardised extract assay than line S. A report concludes, “R must have a mutation that changes catalase's amino acid sequence.”

1. Explain why the conclusion is not established. Give a plausible link from regulation of gene expression to the observations, including a limitation of that explanation.
2. Describe an investigation that compares catalase-associated activity fairly between the lines. Include independent samples, records, controls and an evaluation of the measurement.
3. Explain how conventional endpoint PCR of the catalase gene differs from measuring catalase expression or activity. Include strand separation, primers and extension, and explain why a visible PCR band alone does not establish a difference in expression.
<!-- /task:writing-independent-gene -->

<details markdown="1">
<summary>提示——沿不同层级追踪证据</summary>

题干区分了编码序列、RNA 丰度与酶活性。RNA 减少可能来自产生变少或降解增多。PCR 扩增选定 DNA 片段，不自动测到 RNA 或酶功能。

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>解答——独立综合 A</summary>

> 1. The same catalase coding sequence does not support the proposed change in amino acid sequence from a coding mutation. Reduced transcription could lower catalase mRNA abundance, leading to less translation and potentially less enzyme. For example, altered regulation at a promoter could reduce transcription. However, the observations do not locate a regulatory change: increased mRNA degradation could also lower mRNA abundance, and enzyme activity also depends on translation, protein turnover and assay conditions. A coding-sequence comparison does not exclude changes elsewhere in the genome.
>
> 2. Randomly sample several independent plants from each line at the same developmental stage and extract equal tissue masses into equal volumes of the same buffer. Keep extraction, pH, temperature, extract volume and final hydrogen peroxide concentration comparable, and randomise assay order. Record plant identity, conditions, time and oxygen volume over an early linear interval, then calculate an initial rate for each plant. Include a no-extract blank and check gas collection for leaks. Use independent plant-level results to describe the mean difference and variation; technical repeats improve measurement of each extract but are not extra plants. The assay measures activity per tissue mass, not enzyme concentration directly, and differing inhibitors or extraction recovery may remain. Use eye protection and teacher-prepared dilute peroxide.
>
> 3. In PCR, heating separates DNA strands by disrupting hydrogen bonds between complementary bases. Cooling to a temperature appropriate for the primers allows them to bind to complementary target sequences. A thermostable DNA polymerase extends the primers by joining nucleotides into the new strands, forming phosphodiester bonds; repeated cycles amplify the selected region. The annealing temperature depends on the primers and conditions, so a single example temperature is not universal. An endpoint band indicates amplified DNA of an approximate size, subject to appropriate controls. It does not directly measure catalase mRNA, translation or catalytic activity. Conventional endpoint band intensity is not automatically a reliable measure of starting target amount.

深入机制见[基因表达](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/gene-expression/)和[基因技术](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/gene-technology/)。这里没有声称已测到启动子甲基化；那需要额外证据。若题目问原核生物，也不能把细胞核或真核 pre-mRNA 剪接加入答案。

</details>

<!-- task:writing-independent-evidence -->
**Self-written / 自拟题 · Independent synthesis B**

In a constructed study, equal-aged plants are randomly allocated individually to water treatments. There are 16 independent plants per treatment. Mean final dry mass is 1.80 g with SE 0.10 g for well-watered plants and 1.20 g with SE 0.08 g for drought-treated plants. Stomatal opening was smaller in the drought treatment, but photosynthetic rate was not measured.

Write a continuous English account that quantifies the dry-mass difference, explains a plausible link between water supply, stomata and organic matter production, and evaluates the claim that reduced photosynthesis is the only cause of the difference. Explain what the SE values describe and identify a useful additional measurement.
<!-- /task:writing-independent-evidence -->

<details markdown="1">
<summary>提示——观察结果与路径解释分开</summary>

百分降幅以水分充足组为基值。干质量积累是多个过程的平衡；SE 描述的是均值估计。

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>解答——独立综合 B</summary>

> Drought-treated plants have a mean final dry mass 0.60 g lower, a reduction of about 33.3% relative to the well-watered mean. Reduced water supply can promote stomatal closure, consistent with the smaller observed opening. This can restrict carbon dioxide diffusion into leaves and reduce carbon fixation, limiting production of organic compounds for growth. However, photosynthetic rate was not measured, so the proposed pathway is not directly demonstrated. Final dry mass reflects production, respiration, allocation and any material losses; changes in respiration, leaf area or mineral uptake could also contribute. The SE values estimate uncertainty in the treatment mean estimates under the sampling model, not the spread of individual plant masses. Measuring gas exchange and respiration under defined conditions, alongside initial biomass estimates and leaf area, would help distinguish explanations. Random allocation strengthens inference about the water treatment, but does not identify reduced photosynthesis as the only mediator.

不能仅凭均值 ± SE 条是否重叠作普遍显著性判断；还需要适合的分析假设。相关连接见[光合](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/photosynthesis/)、[呼吸](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/respiration/)和[物质循环](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/nutrient-cycles/)。

</details>

## 找出缺环，再换情境重试 {#retry}

| 回答中的问题 | 具体修复方法 |
| --- | --- |
| “生物因为需要生存，所以改变了” | 自然选择写出已有变异和繁殖差异；生理响应写出细胞过程 |
| 只凭产气量断言“酶更多” | 写清每单位质量、时间的活性指标及替代解释 |
| “This increases it” | 把两个代词改成实体与变量名称，说明方向 |
| 三个主题各有知识点，却无连接 | 写出哪个过程让上一环节的输出成为下一环节的输入 |
| “显著，所以机制解释正确” | 分开统计原假设、效应大小、实验设计与候选机制 |

<!-- task:writing-retry-selection -->
**Self-written / 自拟题 · Retry: population mechanism**

Rewrite this claim as a defensible explanation: “The insects needed resistance, so the pesticide made each insect develop the useful allele.” Assume resistance is heritable and some resistant insects were present before spraying. Explain how allele frequency can change over generations and give one limit to predicting the outcome.
<!-- /task:writing-retry-selection -->

<details markdown="1">
<summary>提示——分清个体与多代种群</summary>

从已有可遗传变异开始，再考虑存活、繁殖和遗传。不要让突变有目的地响应需要。

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>解答——种群机制重试</summary>

> Heritable variation in pesticide resistance existed before spraying. If resistant insects survive treatment more often and produce more surviving offspring, resistance alleles can be passed to a larger fraction of the next generation. Their frequency may therefore increase over generations under this selection pressure. Individual insects do not acquire a directed useful mutation because they need it. The outcome also depends on conditions such as pesticide exposure, resistance costs, migration and chance effects, so resistance need not reach fixation.

修正后把 AS 变异与遗传连接到 A2 [进化](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/evolution/)，描述的是种群变化，不是每个个体都目的性改变。

</details>

<!-- task:writing-retry-comparison -->
**Self-written / 自拟题 · Retry: compare and limit a claim**

Write three or four connected sentences comparing insulin and glucagon in the control of blood glucose. Name their pancreatic source cells, the direction of the response to blood glucose, and a relevant target process. Explain why saying “both hormones make all cells absorb glucose” is incorrect.
<!-- /task:writing-retry-comparison -->

<details markdown="1">
<summary>提示——两种激素比较相同维度</summary>

分清胰岛 beta 与 alpha 细胞，比较适当靶组织中的储存或释放过程，不能把所有葡萄糖转运都归于胰岛素控制。

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>解答——比较重试</summary>

> A rise in blood glucose stimulates pancreatic beta cells to release insulin, whereas a fall stimulates alpha cells to release glucagon. Insulin promotes glucose uptake in responsive tissues such as skeletal muscle and adipose tissue and promotes glycogen synthesis in liver and muscle, lowering blood glucose. Glucagon acts principally on the liver to promote processes such as glycogen breakdown and gluconeogenesis, increasing glucose release into the blood. Their effects therefore oppose each other in glucose regulation; glucagon does not make all cells absorb glucose, and not all cellular glucose uptake depends on insulin.

分别核对来源、靶组织和方向。受体、第二信使与负反馈的详细解释见[稳态](/zh/alevel/a2-biology/homeostasis/)。

</details>
