<h2 id="start-here">先决定怎样解释，再选择方程</h2>

这些**自拟混合任务**连接 CH01 与 CH02，不是官方卷、预测卷或官方评分标准。先完成[学习路线](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/)，再关闭全部帮助作答。计算前说明系统、模型和条件，以英文写连贯科学解释，不只列关键词；题目说明时可用数据表。

记录首次尝试、使用帮助、错误原因及重新作答；学完其他主题后，再做两个不同的[延后新题](#later-check)。即时订正不能证明保持；本页不认证仪器操作能力。

<div class="lesson-check" markdown="1">
<h3 id="mixed-a">一份样品与一种气体</h3>
<!-- task:mixed-a -->
Element X is in Period 3 and has a large jump between its third and fourth ionisation energies. Identify its outer-shell configuration and a plausible element. A 0.540 g alloy contains 80.0% X by mass; the remainder is inert to the excess dilute acid used. X forms X3+(aq), and H+(aq) forms H2(g). Construct the balanced ionic equation and determine the hydrogen volume at 100kPa and 300 K, using Ar(X)=27.0 and R=8.31 J mol−1 K−1. State assumptions needed to compare this dry ideal-gas prediction with an actual wet collected volume. Explain in English why the solid X can conduct, without claiming its ions migrate through the solid.
<!-- /task:mixed-a -->
<details markdown="1"><summary>提示</summary>
先由跃升找外电子，再决定每个金属原子提供几个电子。
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>解答与检查</summary>
第三周期三个外电子为3s² 3p¹，Al。2Al+6H⁺→2Al³⁺+3H₂。Al质量 .540×.800=.432g，n=.0160mol，H₂=.0240mol；V=.0240×8.31×300/100000=.00059832m³=**598cm³**（3位）。需反应完全且可接触、无其他产氢来源、温压正确、干气或校正水蒸气、无显著泄漏／溶解并近似理想。固体金属由离域电子导电，不是离子实穿行。推元素错回[电离证据](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/atomic-structure/#ionisation-evidence)，体积错回[气体](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/formulae-and-gases/)，延后A换体系重试。
</details>
</div>

<div class="lesson-check" markdown="1">
<h3 id="mixed-b">一份实验室报告</h3>
<!-- task:mixed-b -->
An unknown aqueous sample may contain carbonate, sulfate and chloride, separately or together, above the validated detection limits. Design a sequence using separate fresh portions to investigate all three. Explain acid choices, reagent order, visible observations, a gas confirmation, controls and one relevant risk control. Write the relevant ionic equations. A classmate uses HCl before AgNO3, gets a white solid and reports chloride in the original bottle. Evaluate the conclusion and explain why repeating that same procedure does not repair the problem.
<!-- /task:mixed-b -->
<details markdown="1"><summary>提示</summary>
先检查加入试剂会不会自己带来待检离子。
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>解答与检查</summary>
碳酸根：新份加稀酸冒泡，气体通石灰水变浑浊支持 CO₂，避免过量久通；CO₃²⁻+2H⁺→CO₂+H₂O，CO₂+Ca(OH)₂→CaCO₃+H₂O。硫酸根：另份先稀 HCl 除碳酸根，再 BaCl₂ 白色 BaSO₄；Ba²⁺+SO₄²⁻→BaSO₄。氯离子：新份先稀 HNO₃，再 AgNO₃ 白色 AgCl，稀 NH₃ 可溶；Ag⁺+Cl⁻→AgCl。同法空白及已知阳性参考检查试剂与方法。HCl 自带 Cl⁻，不能证明原样有氯，重复只重现污染。护目镜与受监督少量操作防飞溅，Ba/Ag 废物分类。重复用同份的错误回[鉴别决策](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/qualitative-analysis/)，再做延后B。
</details>
</div>

<h3 id="mixed-c">两份能量报告</h3>
<!-- task:mixed-c -->
A rapid aqueous reaction in an open insulated cup has limiting reaction amount 0.0200 mol, total solution mass 75.0 g and corrected temperature rise 4.0 K. Use specific heat capacity 4.18 J g−1 K−1; neglect cup absorption and dilution heat. Determine the enthalpy per mole of the reaction as written and justify its sign and constant-pressure interpretation. Explain the likely direction of error if heat loss from the warm liquid were uncorrected. Separately, given C(graphite)+O2(g)→CO2(g), ΔH=−394 kJ mol−1, and CO(g)+1/2O2(g)→CO2(g), ΔH=−283 kJ mol−1 at the same conditions, construct the equation and enthalpy for forming one mole CO(g). Why cannot these thermochemical numbers be replaced uncritically with a mean gas-phase bond-enthalpy estimate?
<!-- /task:mixed-c -->
<details markdown="1"><summary>提示</summary>
追踪热量方向与摩尔基准，再选择哪条比较式需要反向。
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>解答与检查</summary>
液体 q=75×4.18×4.0=1254J，反应−1.254kJ，ΔH=−62.7≈**−63 kJ mol−1**（温升2位）。敞口杯近似恒压，适当校正且无显著非体积功时反应热对应 ΔH；热损使温升变小、结果不够负。第一式加反向第二式：C石墨+½O₂→CO(g)，−394+283=**−111 kJ mol−1**，CO₂相消。平均键焓是气态平均，非特定环境与物态的准确量；石墨不等于气态单个 C。循环方向错回[Hess](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/hess-and-bond-enthalpies/)，符号／质量错回[量热](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/energetics-and-calorimetry/)，延后A换方向重试。
</details>
</div>

<h3 id="mixed-d">一条曲线与一个密闭容器</h3>
<!-- task:mixed-d -->
A constructed gas-production record gives times 0,10,20,30,40 s and gas volumes 0,18,30,38,40 cm3. Find average volume-production rate over 0–20 s. A tangent at 20 s passes through(15 s,25 cm3) and(25 s,35 cm3): find instantaneous rate there. Distinguish either rate from the final gas amount. Sketch equal-particle-number Maxwell–Boltzmann curves for T2 > T1, mark Ea and explain a temperature increase and a catalyst separately. In another sealed 2.00 dm3 vessel at constant temperature, H2(g)+I2(g)⇌2HI(g) reaches equilibrium: initially 0.100 mol each H2/I2, no HI; equilibrium HI is 0.120 mol. Calculate Kc and its units. The forward reaction is exothermic: predict the effect of higher temperature on Kc and compare adding a catalyst. Explain why equal equilibrium concentrations are unnecessary.
<!-- /task:mixed-d -->
<details markdown="1"><summary>提示</summary>
区分斜率、最终反应量、平衡组成及平衡常数。
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>解答与检查</summary>
平均30/20=**1.50cm³s−1**，切线(35−25)/(25−15)=**1.00cm³s−1**；最终40cm³与量有关，不是速率。MB 总面积相等，高温峰低宽、高能比例更大，升温不改 Ea；催化改路径降 Ea，同温分布不改。HI = .120消耗各.0600mol，H₂/I₂各余.0400mol；浓度 .0200/.0200/.0600，Kc=.0600²/(.0200²)=**9.00**，按浓度约定单位相消。升温有利逆向吸热反应，Kc减小；催化不改Kc及平衡组成。平衡是正逆速率相同，不是浓度相同。用了初始量回[平衡量](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/equilibrium/)，把升温当降Ea回[能量分布](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/kinetics/)，延后B换平衡重试。
</details>
</div>

<h3 id="mixed-e">综合化学式、光谱与反应</h3>
<!-- task:mixed-e -->
An intact singly charged molecular ion has m/z 60.0573. Candidate formulae are C3H8O and C2H4O2. Use relative isotope masses C = 12.0000, H = 1.0078, O = 15.9949. A validated IR record has broad O–H absorption 3200–3550 cm−1 and no C=O band. On oxidation with warm acidified dichromate, the isolated organic product has molecular formula C3H6O, a C=O band and gives negative Tollens under a valid test; remaining candidates are propan-1-ol, propan-2-ol and methoxyethane. Identify the starting structure with a chain of evidence and state what exact mass alone cannot decide. Write its oxidation using[O], choose a halogenoalkane precursor and conditions, then draw the complete mechanism for dehydrating the alcohol to an alkene. Include one reason negative test evidence needs a positive reference. Do not use NMR.
<!-- /task:mixed-e -->
<details markdown="1"><summary>提示</summary>
每项观察缩小一种结构问题；分子式不等于连接方式。
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>解答与检查</summary>
C₃H₈O=**60.0573**，C₂H₄O₂=**60.0210**。IR支持醇、排除醚；氧化为 Tollens 阴性羰基物支持二级 propan-2-ol→propanone，非在不同条件下会形成醛／酸的一级醇。精确质量不能分同分异构；候选集与有效氧化观察均重要。CH₃CH(OH)CH₃+[O]→CH₃COCH₃ + H₂O；前体2-bromopropane，温热水相NaOH有利取代。脱水：酸催化加热，O孤对→H⁺形成 O⁺H₂；C–O键→O脱水成二级碳正离子；水／碱孤对→邻H，C–H对→C–C成propene并再生H⁺。必须画中间体电荷与箭尾。失效试剂可假阴性，已知醛阳性参考检验方法有效；Tollens新鲜配用、依批准监督、不储存。分别回[分析](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/organic-analysis/)、[条件](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/halogenoalkanes/)或[醇机理](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/alcohols/)，延后B换物质。
</details>
</div>

<h3 id="mixed-f">设计两种产物并评价判断</h3>
<!-- task:mixed-f -->
Ethene is used industrially to make epoxyethane. Give the oxidant, catalyst and balanced equation. Draw acid-catalysed ring opening with water and separately methanol, including complete electron-pair arrows, charges, deprotonation and catalyst regeneration. Name/draw the water product and explain a use. A company says “the ring-opening products are useful, so production has no environmental or safety cost”: evaluate with a specific risk and a benefit. Separately draw E and Z forms of CH3CH=C(Cl)CH2CH3 using CIP priorities, and explain why ethene itself has no E/Z pair.
<!-- /task:mixed-f -->
<details markdown="1"><summary>提示</summary>
追踪哪条环键打开、哪种试剂提供新O基；不要混用质子化环与未解释的OH−条件。
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>解答与检查</summary>
2C₂H₄+O₂→2C₂H₄O，高温银催化。环O孤对→H⁺先质子化；水或甲醇O孤对→环C，该C–O环键→环O；新接入氧鎓脱质子再生H⁺。水给HOCH₂CH₂OH（ethane-1,2-diol，防冻剂／原料）；甲醇给HOCH₂CH₂OCH₃，不是三个碳连续成链。下游表面活性剂／防冻用途不能消除环氧乙烷毒性／致癌、易燃爆及能耗排放风险，需密闭受控制造。CIP左CH₃>H，右Cl>乙基，先看直接相连原子序数；Z高优先同侧，E反侧。乙烯每C两个相同H，不满足两取代基不同。机理错回[环氧乙烷](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/epoxyethane/)，优先级错回[有机表征](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/organic-foundations/)。修正后先用乙醇重画开环再核对。
</details>
</div>

<h2 id="later-check">间隔之后的新题</h2>

学习另一主题或学科后，先不重开旧解答，完成A、B；继续记录帮助及错误原因。这是新题，不是抄前面的订正。

<div class="lesson-check" markdown="1">
<h3 id="later-a">延后任务A</h3>
<!-- task:later-a -->
A constructed elemental spectrum has mononuclear 1+ peaks at 63 and 65 with signals 3:1. Find Ar and explain why there need be no peak there. A 0.3980 g hydrated salt loses 0.1800 g water on complete heating without decomposition and leaves 0.2180 g anhydrous salt of molar mass 218.0g mol−1. Find waters per formula unit using 18.0g mol−1. Explain why residual water would affect this inference. Separately, the same final solution state is reached by dissolving anhydrous salt (ΔH1) or its hydrate (ΔH2=+12 kJ mol−1). Hydration anhydrous+nH2O(l)→hydrate has ΔH=−45 kJ mol−1. Construct the Hess relation and find ΔH1; do not infer an actual salt identity from these constructed numbers.
<!-- /task:later-a -->
<details markdown="1"><summary>提示</summary>
同位素用加权；能量循环要让起终态一致。
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>解答与检查</summary>
Ar=(63×3+65)/4=**63.5**，是混合均值不是新同位素。n水=.0100mol，n盐=.00100mol，n=**10**；残水使失重偏小、表观无水盐偏多，水合数偏小。直接溶解等于先水合再溶解水合物：ΔH₁=−45+12=**−33 kJ mol−1**，必须最终溶液状态及水量记账相同。按错误回[加权](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/atomic-structure/#isotope-evidence)、[化学式](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/formulae-and-gases/)或[Hess](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/hess-and-bond-enthalpies/)，收答案换峰比或方向重试。
</details>
</div>

<div class="lesson-check" markdown="1">
<h3 id="later-b">延后任务B</h3>
<!-- task:later-b -->
For a homogeneous 2.00 dm3 equilibrium A(g)⇌2B(g), initial A is 0.100 mol and B is 0; equilibrium B is 0.120 mol. Determine Kc including units and predict the composition shift after compression at the same temperature. Does Kc change? In a separate organic problem an unknown C2H6O has a broad O–H IR band; controlled oxidation with removal of the volatile product gives a Tollens-positive carbonyl. Identify starting compound and carbonyl, write the [O] equation, choose distillation or reflux and justify. In a third fresh aqueous sample, warming with NaOH releases a gas that turns damp red litmus blue. State the supported ion inference, write its ionic equation and explain why the paper must not touch the alkaline liquid. Write one connected English explanation of an evidence limitation across these three investigations.
<!-- /task:later-b -->
<details markdown="1"><summary>提示</summary>
平衡用平衡浓度；气体证据要与液体污染检验分开。
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>解答与检查</summary>
B=.120意味着A反应.0600，余.0400mol；[A]=.0200，[B]=.0600，Kc=.0600²/.0200=**.180 mol dm−3**。压缩有利气体数少的左侧，同温Kc不变。C₂H₆O与OH支持乙醇，受控氧化并蒸出乙醛：CH₃CH₂OH+[O]→CH₃CHO+H₂O；及时移走限制继续氧化，回流留住反应物／产物利于继续反应。Tollens阳性需有效新鲜试剂、参考与候选背景。气体支持NH₄⁺，NH₄⁺+OH⁻→NH₃+H₂O；碱液碰试纸也会蓝，却不能证明放NH₃。英文可说明同温固定Kc而组成可变，化学测试须控制有效，碱污染不能作气体证据；表达不限固定措辞。分别回[平衡](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/equilibrium/)、[制备](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/alcohols/)或[鉴别](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/qualitative-analysis/)，再做相应页新题。
</details>
</div>

<h2 id="after-check">用错误原因决定下一步</h2>

结果正确但模型没有依据，仍需修复。分别检查科学推理、表征、计算及英文清晰度；开过提示就记有帮助练习，之后关帮助再试。按各解答链接回[课程路线](/zh/alevel/as-chemistry/)，不设统一及格比例或固定间隔。范围OxfordAQA9620 AS大纲5.3；任务综合各学习页所列来源及注明构造数据，不冒充官方题分。
